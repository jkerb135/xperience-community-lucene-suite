# Phase 8.1 Plan 05: Editors and Polish Summary

**The rule and synonym editors now follow the Suite conventions:**
- row removes are labelled icon buttons that fit at 669 px;
- conditions are bordered blocks with small level-3 headings;
- errors no longer appear while you are still adding rows. They show inline, under the field or group, once you leave it or a save fails.

This closes every Must and Should finding from 08.1-01.
